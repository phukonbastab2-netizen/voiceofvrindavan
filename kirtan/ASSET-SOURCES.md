# Asset sources

## Included

- **Three.js r180** and its GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils helpers: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180. MIT license is preserved at `/logo/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Voice of Vrindavan logo:** supplied by the owner, displayed without redrawing it.
- **Pandal architecture, cloth geometry, fabric/carpet patterns, decorations, interface and audio synthesis:** authored for this prototype.

## Reference material, not bundled

The owner supplied a photo of a raas stage and a local dance video. These guided the setting and earlier motion studies. Their images, video and soundtrack are not redistributed with this website.

No stock human models, character textures, dance recordings or third-party music recordings are included in this version. Any future uploaded models and animations must carry their own appropriate permissions/notices.
