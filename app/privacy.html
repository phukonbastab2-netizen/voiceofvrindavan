Support, with clear boundaries.

This service is for adults aged 18 and over. It provides general AI-generated information and asynchronous support from the Voice of Vrindavan team. The team is not presented as verified doctors or therapists. This is not diagnosis, medical treatment, a prescription service, or emergency care. AI can make mistakes. Seek qualified professional care for health concerns; do not delay needed care because of a chat reply.

Your conversations

We store your account, chosen concern category, language, messages, AI replies, team replies, support requests and timestamps. Passwords and recovery codes are stored as protected hashes. We use an essential session cookie. Cloudflare hosts the service and processes a limited recent portion of your chat to generate AI replies. Avoid sharing identity documents, payment details or unnecessary identifying information.

Messages are private from other members, but readable by the service operator and authorized team. They are not end-to-end encrypted. AI and human replies are labelled. Asking the team adds your conversation to an inbox; it does not guarantee immediate review, a response time, or clinical expertise.

Retention and control

Support messages older than 30 days are excluded from chat access and removed in bounded cleanup during service activity. Inactive support threads expire after 30 days. You can download a retained chat, delete a chat, or delete your account in the app. Deletion removes the corresponding active database records. Cloud-provider recovery copies may have their own retention. Files you download remain on your device until you delete them.

These support conversations are separate from the old community dataset. They are not included in the legacy Google Drive exporter or AI-training exports. We do not use these conversations to train our own models. Sending a message allows processing needed to provide this chat; it is not permission for public sharing.

Urgent help

This app is not continuously monitored for emergencies. Automated urgency flags can miss a crisis or flag one incorrectly. In immediate danger or with severe symptoms, seek urgent in-person help. In India, call 112 for emergencies or 14416 for Tele-MANAS mental-health support. Elsewhere contact your local emergency or crisis service. Do not wait for this app.

Official resources: India Emergency Response Support System · Tele-MANAS.

Respectful use

Do not harass, threaten, impersonate others, share someone else’s private information, or use this service for unlawful activity. Pilot usage limits apply. Accounts violating these rules may be suspended. For a privacy concern, request the team in your chat. Existing community records remain subject to the earlier community privacy notice.
