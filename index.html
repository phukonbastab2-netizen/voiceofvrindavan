BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things
to know.

Will it work on my phone?

Your phone needs Android 8 or newer, ARM64 and OpenGL ES 3.2. Meeting these requirements does not guarantee performance. Real-device testing is still outstanding; this build is not available for iPhone.

Is this the final game?

No. This first prototype contains a small exploration and construction loop with placeholder terrain, character and buildings. Finished art, multiplayer, residents and expanded missions are not included.

What if Android says “App not installed”?

Check that the download completed, your phone meets the requirements and there is enough free storage. If Android reports a security block, keep its protections enabled and record the exact message. Do not uninstall an existing game copy without first considering its saved progress.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved locally on your device after construction. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove it. There is no cloud-save feature in this prototype.

Is the valley a historical reconstruction?

No. The current valley layout and structures are fictional placeholders, not a documented reconstruction of historical Rajneeshpuram.
